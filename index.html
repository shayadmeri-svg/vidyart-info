What is VidyaRT?

VidyaRT is a student practice platform for JEE and NEET preparation. It brings together full-length mock tests, subject practice, step-by-step question reviews, and performance reports.

What can I practise on VidyaRT?

Explore mock tests, subject papers, and focused practice in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology. Choose the subjects and practice modes that match your exam and current study goals.

How do the performance reports help?

Reports help you review accuracy, time spent, chapter performance, and answer patterns. Use the questions and worked solutions behind a result to decide what to revise next.

Does VidyaRT have a JEE Main rank predictor?

Yes. VidyaRT offers a JEE Main rank estimate as a planning tool alongside your practice results. You need enough answers across the required subjects to unlock it. Estimates are not official ranks or guarantees of exam results.

Where do I start?

Open the VidyaRT student app, sign in, and choose an available practice paper. After your attempt, review the report and worked solutions to plan your next session.
