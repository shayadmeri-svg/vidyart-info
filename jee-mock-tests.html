Choose a mock that fits your preparation

A full paper and a chapter test answer different questions. Choose the session that matches what you need to learn.

  • Full mock: practise staying focused across subjects and managing the clock.
  • Subject test: give Physics, Chemistry, or Maths a closer look.
  • Chapter practice: revisit one idea before testing it in a mixed paper.
  • Check the paper’s syllabus, marking rules, and time limit before you begin. Available papers depend on your account or institute.

Your score is the start of the review

Use your report to separate what you know from what you rushed, guessed, or need to learn again.

  • Physics: check the setup, units, and assumptions before blaming the calculation.
  • Chemistry: identify whether the gap is recall, a concept, or a calculation.
  • Mathematics: find the first step where your method stopped working.
  • Choose one or two weak chapters for your next session, then compare another attempt.

A practical test–review–retry routine

Give the review its own place in your study plan. Finishing another paper is less useful if the same mistake keeps returning.

  • Attempt a paper under its stated conditions.
  • Review incorrect, skipped, and guessed answers with the worked solutions.
  • Write down the concept or step you missed, in your own words.
  • Practise that concept again, then return to a mixed test.

Questions, answered.

How do I start a JEE mock test on VidyaRT?

Sign in to the student app and choose an available paper. Check its subjects, instructions, and timing before you start. Your available tests may depend on your institute or account.

Should I take a full mock or practise a chapter?

Choose a full mock to work on overall pace and exam stamina. Choose chapter practice when you can name a specific concept or method that needs work.

Does VidyaRT guarantee an IIT rank?

No. VidyaRT provides practice, solutions, and performance reports. Its JEE Main rank estimate does not guarantee an exam rank or admission.
